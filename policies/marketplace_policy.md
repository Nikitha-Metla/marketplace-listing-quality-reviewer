# Marketplace Listing Policy

## POLICY-01: Required Information

Every listing must contain:

- Title
- Description
- Category
- Price
- Seller

Missing required information must be reported.

---

## POLICY-02: Accurate Information

Listing information must accurately describe the
product or service.

Sellers must not provide false or misleading
information.

---

## POLICY-03: Misleading Claims

Listings must not contain:

- Unsupported guarantees
- False claims
- Unverifiable performance claims
- Claims presented as facts without evidence

Examples:

- "100% guaranteed results"
- "Works for everyone"
- "Guaranteed to make you rich"

---

## POLICY-04: Prohibited Content

Listings must not promote prohibited goods,
illegal services, or restricted activities.

The reviewer should flag suspicious content
rather than attempting to determine legality.

---

## POLICY-05: Title Quality

Titles should:

- Clearly describe the item
- Avoid excessive promotional language
- Avoid unnecessary capitalization
- Avoid misleading claims

Maximum recommended title length: 120 characters.

---

## POLICY-06: Description Quality

Descriptions should:

- Clearly explain the product/service
- Include important characteristics
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Avoid excessive promotional language

Maximum recommended description length: 5000 characters.

---

## POLICY-07: Price

Price must be:

- Numeric
- Greater than zero
- Represented in a valid currency format

Negative or zero prices are invalid.

---

## POLICY-08: Category

Listings must belong to a supported marketplace
category.

Supported categories:

- Electronics
- Home
- Clothing
- Books
- Beauty
- Services
- Sports
- Accessories
---

## POLICY-09: Duplicate Listings

Listings should not duplicate an existing marketplace listing
with the same normalized title.

Duplicate listings should be reviewed before approval.
