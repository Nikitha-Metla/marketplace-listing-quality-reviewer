# Brand Content Guide

## BRAND-01: Clear Language

Use concise and understandable language.

Avoid unnecessary repetition.

---

## BRAND-02: Professional Tone

Avoid:

- Excessive emojis
- Excessive capitalization
- Aggressive sales language
- Repeated punctuation

---

## BRAND-03: Evidence-Based Claims

Claims should be supported by product information.

Avoid phrases such as:

- "Best in the world"
- "#1 product"
- "Guaranteed"
- "Never fails"
- "Works instantly"

unless appropriate evidence is available.

---

## BRAND-04: Helpful Descriptions

Descriptions should explain:

- What the item is
- Important features
- Intended use
- Relevant specifications

---

## BRAND-05: No Manipulative Language

Avoid:

- "Buy now or regret it!"
- "Only stupid people don't buy this!"
- "Guaranteed to change your life!"
